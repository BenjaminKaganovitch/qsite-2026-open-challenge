# [S14] Perturbative floating phase

**Citation:** A. K. Chandra and S. Dasgupta, “Floating phase in a 1D transverse ANNNI model,” Phys. Rev. E 75, 021105 (2007), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.75.021105; arXiv:cond-mat/0612144. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0612144).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full 11-page preprint.

## Claims we will use

Perturbation about the classical multiphase point predicts an extended floating regime, including into κ<.5. This is a method-dependent historical result and is in tension with later numerical phase diagrams.

## Equations and conventions

Expansion variable r=J(κ−.5)/Γ; first-order characteristic bounds r=−.5 and1. Classical multiphase configurations exclude isolated domains of length one.

## Parameters

Large approximate calculations include N1000, checks atN12; perturbative validity assessed through correction sizes.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Do not treat a first-order small-field prediction as an exact global boundary or evidence that the challenge’s small noisy ring resolves it.

## Rubric relevance

Physical insight, limitations.
