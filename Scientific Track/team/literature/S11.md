# [S11] QCNN and ANNNI VQE paper

**Citation:** S. Monaco, O. Kiss, A. Mandarino, S. Vallecorsa and M. Grossi, “Quantum phase detection generalization from marginal quantum neural network models,” Phys. Rev. B 107, L081105 (2023), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.107.L081105; arXiv:2208.08748. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.08748).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full 9-page preprint including VQE appendix.

## Claims we will use

Marginal/axis training can generalize phase classification. VQE energy agreement does not ensure accurate wavefunctions near criticality or degeneracy.

## Equations and conventions

RY layers with linear CNOT entanglers prepare real states. Original QCNN output encoding is 01 ferro,10 para,11 antiphase,00 unused; this differs from later demo and repository label maps.

## Parameters

N=6,12; VQE depth6,9 respectively; five training rounds of1000 updates, Adam learning rates reduced .3→.1, neighboring-parameter reuse. Relative energy errors below1% coexist with low-field fidelities around .5 and poorer critical-region performance.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Approximate-reference classification accuracy is not an independent thermodynamic phase-boundary measurement. No gate-noise ranking of ANNNI phases follows from this clean study.
