## Limitations and unresolved points

Fidelity is squared Uhlmann fidelity, reducing to ⟨ψ|ρ|ψ⟩ against a pure clean state. This compares to noiseless Trotter evolution, not exact exp(−iHt). Public helper gate-set and fidelity assertions are challenge-specific, not ANNNI validation.
