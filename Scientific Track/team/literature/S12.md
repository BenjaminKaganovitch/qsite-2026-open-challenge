# [S12] ANNNI floating phase and autoencoder study

**Citation:** M. Cea et al., “Exploring the Phase Diagram of the quantum one-dimensional ANNNI model,” arXiv:2402.11022v1 (2024). [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11022).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full 15-page v1; quantum-learning and TN comparisons.

## Claims we will use

Large-system tensor-network diagnostics resolve floating behavior; small quantum classifiers struggle. Lower antiphase–floating transition is PT; upper floating–para transition is KT. Autoencoder diagrams show no floating region through N=12; at18 it is less sharply resolved than ordered phases.

## Equations and conventions

OBC H=−ΣXX+κΣNNN XX−hΣZ. Conformal entropy fits use open-chain coefficient c/6. Friedel oscillations in transverse Z (starter X) are not interchangeable with longitudinal SZZ(q).

## Parameters

## Limitations and unresolved points

No guarantee N=8–12 PBC can resolve a floating phase. Axis labels do not teach a missing fourth class. Ground states are tensor-network/ED-prepared, not a noisy VQE demonstration.
