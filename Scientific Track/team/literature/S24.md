# [S24] QuSpin sparse construction

**Citation:** P. Weinberg and M. Bukov, “QuSpin: a Python package for dynamics and exact diagonalisation of quantum many body systems. Part I: spin chains,” SciPost Phys. 2, 003 (2017), doi:10.21468/SciPostPhys.2.1.003. [Source](https://scipost.org/SciPostPhys.2.1.003).

**Access/read status:** FULL. All 41 pages read, including dynamics examples, full example code, appendices and references.

## Claims we will use

The complete paper was read. Operator strings and site-coupling lists build sparse Hamiltonians with optional symmetry sectors.

## Equations and conventions

pauli=False selects spin operators S=σ/2; default Pauli convention matches challenge operators. PBC uses index moduloN. eigsh extracts partial spectrum; eigh returns full eigensystem.

## Parameters

Sec.2.1 example L12 OBC XXZ, Jxy=√2,Jzz=1,hz=1/√3. Do not copy its fixed total-magnetization sector into transverse-field ANNNI: that symmetry is absent.

The examples also demonstrate time evolution, Floquet operators, entropy and observable measurements. For a ground-state sparse solve use the smallest algebraic eigenvalue, not the smallest magnitude; targeting an interior eigenvalue by shift-invert is a different task.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Historical paper installation instructions are not evidence of Python3.14 compatibility. Some displayed example field lists omit the final site; construct from our stated Hamiltonian and validate.
