# [S17] Critical entanglement entropy

**Citation:** P. Calabrese and J. Cardy, “Entanglement entropy and quantum field theory,” J. Stat. Mech. P06002 (2004), doi:10.1088/1742-5468/2004/06/P06002; arXiv:hep-th/0405152v3. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0405152).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full33-page v3, including correction and appendix.

## Claims we will use

Single-interval entropy gives a central-charge diagnostic in the conformal scaling regime. The 2008 note corrects the general multiple-interval result; it does not invalidate the single contiguous half-chain formula used here.

## Equations and conventions

Pure ground state, natural logs: Sℓ=(c/3)ln[(N/(πa))sin(πℓ/N)]+const for PBC. OBC attached interval has c/6 coefficient and boundary constants. Infinite gapped half-chain scaling S≈(c/6)ln(ξ/a).

## Parameters

Ising c=1/2; c=1 critical examples. Applicability requires lengths large compared with lattice spacing.

## Limitations and unresolved points

N8–12 alone is too short to certify central charge. Reduced entropy of a globally mixed noisy state includes classical mixing and is not an entanglement measure.
