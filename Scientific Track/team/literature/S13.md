# [S13] DMRG high-frustration phase diagram

**Citation:** M. Beccaria, M. Campostrini and A. Feo, “Numerical study of the axial next-nearest-neighbor Ising model in a transverse magnetic field,” Phys. Rev. B 76, 094410 (2007), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.76.094410; arXiv:cond-mat/0702676. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0702676).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full 20-page preprint; Eq.(18), tables and finite-size analysis.

## Claims we will use

Combines domain-wall, gap, entropy and correlation diagnostics. Eq.(18) is the source of the commonly reused two high-frustration fitted curves; their coefficient1.05 is empirical.

## Equations and conventions

hLower≈1.05(κ−.5); hUpper≈1.05√[(κ−.5)(κ−.1)]. Upper KT correlation length can become enormous. A vanishing first gap inside an ordered phase can arise from degeneracy.

## Parameters

κ=.5,.52,.55,.6,.75,1,1.25,1.5,2,5; exact small systems and DMRG up to order10³ sites. κ=.75: lower .2574(2), upper gap .424(3), entropy .44(1).

## Limitations and unresolved points

Fits are thermodynamic guides, not exact analytical phase labels. Some fits discard very short chains; no calibrated universal N=8–12 PBC error bar follows.
