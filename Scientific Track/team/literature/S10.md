# [S10] DMRG low-frustration and disorder line

**Citation:** M. Beccaria, M. Campostrini and A. Feo, “Density Matrix Renormalization Group Study of the Disorder Line in the Quantum ANNNI Model,” Phys. Rev. B 73, 052402 (2006), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.73.052402; arXiv:cond-mat/0511658v1. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0511658).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full 4-page preprint; Table I, Eqs.(1)–(5).

## Claims we will use

OBC DMRG resolves the Ising transition and the modulation onset inside the gapped paramagnet. Table I distinguishes numerical critical fields from second-order estimates. Thus oscillatory correlations alone do not establish a floating phase.

## Equations and conventions

H=−J1ΣZZ−J2ΣNNN ZZ−BΣX; κ=−J2/J1, h=B/J1. Czz(d)≈c0 exp(−d/r) cos(πqd+φ). Be careful: downloaded v1 prints Eq.(4)/(5) signs inconsistent with positive-κ table values and later convention; do not copy those signs blindly.

## Parameters
