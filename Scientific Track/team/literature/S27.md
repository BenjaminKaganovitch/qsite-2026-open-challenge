## Claims we will use

 Backprop differentiates analytic simulator execution; parameter-shift and finite differences are circuit transforms. Differentiability must be assessed for chosen operations, measurements and interface.

## Equations and conventions

For fixed-noise channels and ordinary rotation parameters, shift-rule linearity survives channel composition. Finite-difference is an approximate numerical fallback. Neither fact makes every arbitrary returned density-matrix function shift-rule compatible.

## Parameters

Use shots=None for analytic mixed-device backprop; finite-shot expectations may use supported parameter-shift. Pin0.44.1 and explicitly test chosen interface later.
