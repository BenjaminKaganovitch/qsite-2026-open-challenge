## Claims we will use

Explains templates, explicit lattice edges, custom couplings and manually assembled Pauli sums. The lattice graph defines which bonds exist; a model name alone does not fix the challenge’s signs or periodic wrapping.

## Equations and conventions

Ising example −JΣZZ−hΣX uses Pauli matrices. Manual construction sums one term for each selected edge. Spin operators S=σ/2 would instead introduce factors 1/4 in two-site terms and 1/2 in fields.

## Parameters

Examples include square 2×2 Fermi–Hubbard, Heisenberg couplings [0.5,0.5,0.5], honeycomb TFIM J=0.5,h=1, and custom 3×3 lattices.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Not a dedicated ANNNI constructor or validation of small-ring duplicate-bond conventions. Fermionic templates have different degrees of freedom.
