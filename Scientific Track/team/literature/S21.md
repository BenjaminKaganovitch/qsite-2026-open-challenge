# [S21] Symmetry verification

**Citation:** X. Bonet-Monroig, R. Sagastizabal, M. Singh and T. E. O’Brien, “Low-cost error mitigation by symmetry verification,” Phys. Rev. A 98, 062339 (2018), doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.98.062339; arXiv:1807.10050. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.10050).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full11-page preprint; Eq.(13).

## Claims we will use

Known symmetry sectors enable rejection of detectable errors, either by physical verification or reconstructed expectation values. Symmetry-preserving errors remain invisible.

## Equations and conventions

For P²=I, target parity s, Πs=(I+sP)/2. If [O,P]=0: ⟨O⟩s=(⟨O⟩+s⟨OP⟩)/(1+s⟨P⟩). Challenge parity P=ΠiXi.

## Parameters

Need measured parity, joint OP expectation, and nonzero sector weight. Analytic mixed-state simulation can evaluate these without a shot-based postselection circuit.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Correct target sector must be established; h=0 degeneracy needs care. High-weight OP can be costly on hardware. Better state fidelity need not monotonically improve every observable or energy.
