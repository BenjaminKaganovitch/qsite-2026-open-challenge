## Claims we will use

Entropy contraction plus classical Gibbs-state methods yields bounds on achievable optimization advantage. Matching energy with a Gibbs state does not establish equality of all observables or make the noisy output a thermal state.

## Equations and conventions

For uniform full layers of local replacement depolarization ε, D(Φ(ρ)||I/2^n)≤(1−ε)^(2D)n, with base convention fixed. Here ε=4pPL/3. Local preparation of substantial distant correlations requires depth related to their separation.
