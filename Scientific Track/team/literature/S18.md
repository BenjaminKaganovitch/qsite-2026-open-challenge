# [S18] Noise-induced barren plateaus

**Citation:** S. Wang et al., “Noise-induced barren plateaus in variational quantum algorithms,” Nat. Commun. 12, 6961 (2021), doi:10.1038/s41467-021-27045-6; arXiv:2007.14384v6 (2024). [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14384).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full27-page v6 including supplementary proofs; do not conflate with original-version constants.

## Claims we will use

Local Pauli noise can flatten the whole cost landscape exponentially with noisy depth; this differs from random-initialization barren plateaus. Rescaling gradients also rescales sampling uncertainty.

## Equations and conventions

v6 defines q=max|qσ|<1 and c=1/(2ln2); gradient bound has polynomial prefactor times q^(cL+1). Local depolarization has q=|1−4pPL/3|. Theorem assumes noise on all qubits between unitary layers.
