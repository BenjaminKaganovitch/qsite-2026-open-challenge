## Claims we will use

Provides an actual hardware-efficient VQE example, an Ising coupling sweep, a small square lattice, and Trotterized dynamics. Its pedagogical magnetization picture must be corrected for finite symmetry-preserving exact ground states.

## Equations and conventions

OBC chain H=−JΣZZ−hxΣX; HEA single-qubit rotation triples before/after a nearest-neighbour CNOT chain. Mz=N⁻¹ΣZ. Dynamical rate γ(t)=−N⁻¹ ln|⟨ψi|ψ(t)⟩|².

## Parameters

Initial chain example three qubits, J=2, ground energy −4 at zero transverse field; later finite square lattice and 50 time samples.

## Limitations and unresolved points

The infinite-chain critical ratio J/hx=1 does not create a finite-N thermodynamic discontinuity. Raw |⟨Mz⟩| is sensitive to VQE symmetry breaking. A cusp-like finite plot alone does not establish a dynamical transition.
