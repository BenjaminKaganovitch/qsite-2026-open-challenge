# [S28] Our derivation: correlator damping and what it can predict

**Citation:** Q-SITE scientific team, “Depolarizing-channel response of ANNNI diagnostics,” literature derivation (2026-09-25), based on the Kraus convention in [S26](S26.md). Prepared with an AI coding assistant; not a published result or numerical experiment.

**Status:** Original algebra. Assumptions are explicit below. Rubric: noise depth, physical insight, technical sophistication.

## Channel convention

For a channel on qubit t,

\[
\mathcal D_{p,t}(\rho)=(1-p)\rho+\frac p3\sum_{\sigma=X,Y,Z}\sigma_t\rho\sigma_t,
\qquad a=1-\frac{4p}{3}.
\]

The adjoint fixes I and sends X,Y,Z to a times themselves. This follows because one of the three conjugations preserves a given nonidentity Pauli and two reverse its sign. Equivalently, on a single qubit, Dp(ρ)=aρ+(1−a)I/2. For a subsystem of an entangled state the replacement term is I_t/2 ⊗ Tr_tρ, not the whole-system maximally mixed state. This is a convex replacement interpretation only for p≤3/4, which covers our experiment.

## Exact terminal-channel benchmark

Assume the noiseless state is fully prepared first and then each site gets m independent identical channels, with no later entangling gates. A weight-w Pauli string is multiplied by a^(mw). Thus, for i≠j,

\[
C_{ij}'=a^{2m}C_{ij},\quad C_{ii}'=1,\quad
\langle X_i\rangle'=a^m\langle X_i\rangle,\quad
\langle Z_i\rangle'=a^m\langle Z_i\rangle.
\]

Connected distinct-site correlations also scale by a^(2m), because both one-point factors scale by a^m. With unequal terminal counts mi,mj use a^(mi+mj).

Define S(q)=N⁻¹Σij exp[iq(i−j)]Cij and m²(q)=S(q)/N. Then

\[
S'(q)=1+a^{2m}[S(q)-1],\qquad
m'^2(q)=\frac1N+a^{2m}\left[m^2(q)-\frac1N\right].
\]

Self-correlations create the floor; multiplying the whole S(q) by a² is wrong. For one terminal layer:

| p | a | a² |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| .01 | .9866667 | .9735111 |
| .05 | .9333333 | .8711111 |

Uniform terminal noise leaves the maximizing q unchanged, and leaves ratios of nonzero off-diagonal correlators unchanged. It attenuates ferro and period-four two-point signals by the same factor; a stronger intrinsic damping exponent for antiphase does not follow from its visual pattern. A true distinct-site four-point string scales as a^(4m). The starter product of pair expectations also happens to scale as a^(4m) in this special terminal model, without becoming a genuine four-point observable.

For a globally mixed state, reduced von Neumann entropy is not entanglement entropy. Terminal local depolarization cannot decrease the reduced entropy on a fixed subsystem, by unitality. This statement does not assign a universal response to an arbitrary interleaved circuit followed by more entangling gates.

## Actual CNOT-target noise

Write the actual circuit as U1,D1,U2,D2,…,UG,DG, where Dg is present only after a CNOT and acts on its target. Propagate an observable backwards through the circuit. At each channel, every Pauli component nontrivial on that target gains a factor a; at a unitary, conjugate the observable.

For a Clifford circuit and a single Pauli observable, conjugation preserves a single Pauli string, so the exact attenuation is a^m(P), where m(P) counts nontrivial target encounters in this **backward trajectory**. It is not simply the number of noisy gates times the output Pauli weight. For example, after one noisy CNOT, Z_control is unaffected by target noise while Z_control Z_target gets one factor a, not a².

With generic RY angles, backward propagation produces a sum of Pauli components with different exponents and coefficients. CNOT propagation and rotations make a universal two-point damping factor invalid even if every site is targeted once per ring layer.

An exact first-order response for a fixed circuit and parameters is

\[
\left.\frac{d\langle O\rangle_p}{dp}\right|_0
=\sum_{g\in\mathrm{CNOTs}}\mathrm{Tr}\{O_g\,\mathcal L_{t_g}(\rho_g)\},
\quad
\mathcal L_t(\rho)=\frac13\sum_{\sigma=X,Y,Z}\sigma_t\rho\sigma_t-\rho.
\]

Here ρg is the ideal state just after gate g and Og is the final observable pulled backwards through all later ideal gates. Each summand can have either sign. This formula predicts a response once the preparation circuit and measurement are specified; it does not require fitting noisy data. Parameter reoptimization adds the term ∇θ⟨O⟩·dθ*/dp, which is not fixed by channel algebra.

## Boundary displacement

Let a boundary estimator solve f(h,p)=0 and assume a simple crossing with ∂hf≠0. Then

\[
\delta h=-p\frac{\partial_p f(h_0,0)}{\partial_h f(h_0,0)}+O(p^2).
\]

For a fixed threshold T above a noise floor b and terminal contraction f=b+a^w[O0(h)−b]−T, if O0 decreases with h at the crossing, the shift is toward **lower h**. This predicts shrinkage for that particular threshold rule. If the boundary is the location of a derivative maximum under a parameter-independent affine rescaling, there is **no shift**. A crossing S(0)=S(π/2) is also unchanged under uniform terminal contraction. Nonuniform/interleaved noise and retraining need the full response formula.

| Diagnostic | Defensible advance prediction | Unsupported inference |
|---|---|---|
| Ferro/antiphase two-point scores | Terminal contrasts shrink equally by weight; threshold shifts conditional on slope | Antiphase must always be more fragile |
| q* and correlation ratios | Invariant under uniform terminal contraction | Floating boundaries must move in a particular direction |
| Transverse magnetization | Terminal magnitude contracts | A noisy unpolarized state is a field-polarized paramagnet |
| Hamiltonian spectrum/gap | Unchanged: noise is in preparation, not H | Gate noise literally changes the equilibrium Hamiltonian critical field |
| Neighboring-state fidelity | A common CPTP map cannot reduce Uhlmann fidelity; metric response contracts under that condition | Differently parameterized interleaved circuits form the same common map |
| Half-chain entropy | Terminal mixing can increase it | Increased noisy entropy proves stronger entanglement or c=1 |
| QCNN probabilities/AD loss | Circuit-specific response; class margins may decrease | Universal sign of every class-boundary displacement |

The requested “direction of each boundary shift” is therefore mathematically underdetermined without a circuit and estimator. Record these conditional predictions before implementation; do not replace them with a predetermined robustness ranking.

## Mitigation consequences

For the terminal benchmark only, invert S−1 by a^(−2m); finite-shot variances grow by the square of the inverse factor. A composed-channel scale λ corresponds to pλ=(3/4)[1−(1−4p/3)^λ]. Direct p→λp is a different scaling path, useful perturbatively while λp≤1. For actual CNOT-target noise, hold ideal gates and parameters fixed and scale every specified noise location consistently. No numerical noise simulations were run for this note.
