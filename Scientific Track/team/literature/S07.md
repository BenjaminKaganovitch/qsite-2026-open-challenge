## Equations and conventions

build_annni_hamiltonian: −j1ΣZZ+j1κΣNNN ZZ−hΣX. PBC default: N bonds of each kind; OBC N−1 and N−2. Fields measured in j1 units only when h/j1 is used. observables computes pure-state vdot expectations. z_abs_mean is mean_i|⟨Zi⟩|, not ⟨|Mz|⟩. antiphase_string_mean is mean_i[⟨ZiZi+1⟩⟨Zi+2Zi+3⟩], not a four-point expectation.

## Parameters

ED uses full np.linalg.eigh and caches dense local operators. noise circuit shares a single scalar θ across RY gates; default two layers, sequential ring CNOTs, target DepolarizingChannel(p), default.mixed. Plot arrays [h,κ].

## Limitations and unresolved points

Observable NN/NNN summaries wrap around even if a supplied state came from OBC. Antiphase proxy can be large in ferro and vanish in a translated antiphase cat. reference.py returns 0 at κ=0 due denominator safeguard plus cancellation; true Ising limit is 1. E1−E0 may be a symmetry doublet splitting. No starter edits made.
