# [S30] Hamiltonian variational ansatz (added during implementation)

**Citation:** D. Wecker, M. B. Hastings and M. Troyer, "Towards Practical Quantum Variational Algorithms," arXiv:1507.08969v2 [quant-ph] (2015). [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08969).

**Access/read status:** SCOPED. The v2 text was retrieved on 2026-09-25 and only the title, authors, abstract and introduction were read, to attribute the method. The journal version was not inspected.

## Claims we will use

The paper proposes a class of variational states motivated by adiabatic state preparation: alternating exponentials of the terms of the problem Hamiltonian, applied to an easily prepared initial state. That is the ansatz family our HVA belongs to: |+>^N followed by layers of exp(-i a ZZ_nn), exp(-i c ZZ_nnn) and exp(-i b X). The paper's test systems are Hubbard ladders and small molecules, not ANNNI, so it supplies no ANNNI accuracy or depth prediction.

## Use in this project

This note attributes the ansatz idea only. Every accuracy statement for our circuit (depth choice, energy and fidelity errors) comes from our own measurements in `team/results/analysis/prep_quality_N8_L4.json` and `depth_tradeoff.json`.
