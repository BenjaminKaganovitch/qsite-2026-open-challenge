# [S15] Ground-state overlap as a transition diagnostic

**Citation:** P. Zanardi and N. Paunković, “Ground state overlap and quantum phase transitions,” Phys. Rev. E 74, 031123 (2006), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.74.031123; arXiv:quant-ph/0512249. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0512249).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full 5-page preprint.

## Claims we will use

Ground-state overlap is sensitive to restructuring near quantum criticality without prespecifying an order parameter. The examples are XY/Dicke, not a calibration of ANNNI floating boundaries.

## Equations and conventions

Amplitude overlap f=|⟨ψ(λ)|ψ(λ+δ)⟩| differs from squared fidelity F=f². State phase choices disappear under modulus, but rotations within a degenerate subspace do not.

## Parameters

Neighboring coupling points; specify the displacement direction and step for a two-parameter ANNNI map.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Finite-size dips, level changes and small overlaps alone do not prove a thermodynamic phase. Use gap/structure-factor cross-checks.
