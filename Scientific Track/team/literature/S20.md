# [S20] Variational simulation and active error reduction

**Citation:** Y. Li and S. C. Benjamin, “Efficient variational quantum simulator incorporating active error minimization,” Phys. Rev. X 7, 021050 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.7.021050; arXiv:1611.09301v5. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09301).

**Access/read status:** FULL. Full14-page v5 with appendices.

## Claims we will use

Stochastic error boosting permits extrapolation of measured expectations to zero error. Uniform multiplicative error can cancel in a variational equation, but nonuniform error generally does not.

## Equations and conventions

M λdot=V; linear fit ⟨X⟩(r)=⟨X⟩0+χr+O(r²). Model single-qubit channel (1−ε)ρ+(ε/3)ΣPρP matches PennyLane p=ε.

## Parameters

Three-spin periodic Ising dynamics, J=B=.5; two-qubit error.1%, other errors one tenth; demonstrated scales1,2; shot studies10⁴without and10⁶with mitigation.

## Limitations and unresolved points

Short-circuit perturbative extrapolation is not reliable when total error burden becomes large. Dynamics example does not establish ANNNI boundary recovery.
